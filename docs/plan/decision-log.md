## 2026-10-06 · Scanned-page transcription and card English gloss [M1-24]
- Context: M1-24 (P1) adds `page-transcriber@1` with J19 `transcribe-pages` (07 §7.2.2), and `knowledge-gloss@1` with `requestCardGloss` (05 §5.4).
- Decision:

Cached in `gloss_en` with the model, time and a hash of the card text; `getCardWithEvidence` returns it with `stale` when the card changed since. Not made for English cards; a card of a source needs the source's AI-processing right. **Never approved content:** it is not in `snapshotOf`, `knowledge_item_versions`, the embedding text, retrieval results or `CardView` (tests), and the card screen shows it in its own panel labelled "Not approved text".

## 2026-10-06 · Source chunks and source search [M1-21]
- Context: M1-21 (P1) adds chunks of the raw source text with vectors, and a "search sources" panel in the card editor (06 §6.3 step 6, 04 `source_chunks`).
- Decision:
  - **Chunker** (`ingestion/chunking.ts`, pure): sentences are the unit; chunks of about 800 tokens, the last sentences (up to 100 tokens) open the next chunk; a sentence longer than a chunk is cut by words, a word longer than a chunk by characters. Tokens are estimated like the extraction planner (3 characters per token for Cyrillic languages, 4 otherwise). Each chunk keeps its first page, last page, section and a SHA-256 of its text.
  - **Index** (`embedding/chunks.ts` `indexSourceChunks`): chunks of the source's current attempt replace the old rows in one transaction; a chunk with the same text hash and embedding model keeps its vector, so running it again costs nothing. `ingest-source` calls it after READY; a failure is only logged (the cards are the product, search is optional). `rc index-chunks [sourceId]` indexes the ready sources that allow AI processing (for sources ingested before this task); it calls the embedding provider, so with `AI_PROVIDER=live` it costs a few cents per guide.
  - **Search** (`searchSourceChunks`): the question is embedded, cosine order, archived sources left out, optional single source, at most 30 hits. The panel on the card screen shows source, pages, section, a match percentage and the passage; "Only in this source" narrows it to the card's source. Chunks are made only after the AI-rights gate passed, so a source that forbids AI processing has no chunks and never appears in the search.
- Evidence / links: `ingestion/chunking.test.ts`, `embedding/chunks.test.ts`, `ingest-source.test.ts`.
- Impact on plan: M1-21 ticked. The panel does not yet open the PDF at the found page; the sources screen (M1-04) can link there.

## 2026-10-03 · Retrieval service [M1-19]
- Context: M1-19 asks for `candidatePool()`, `searchApproved()` and `getIdeaCards(ideaId)` (07 §7.9.1). The tables for ideas (`master_ideas`, `master_idea_knowledge`) come with M2-01, so what depends on them cannot be built yet.
- Decision: `modules/src/knowledge/retrieval/`.
  - **`candidatePool(ctx, options)`:** approved (`CHEF_APPROVED`) cards that have a vector, filtered by categories and language, newest approval first (at most 2,000 are considered); cards in `recentlyUsedIds` are left out unless that leaves fewer than `minPool` (40), in which case the exclusion is skipped and `exclusionApplied` says so; then at most 60 cards by MMR (λ 0.7, `mmr.ts`) over the vectors. `categoryWeights` multiply relevance per category (above 1 for a coverage gap, below 1 for an overused category; both inputs come from the idea stage and later from performance memory). Without a query the relevance of a card is its weight, so the first pick is the most relevant one and the rest are chosen for difference; ties go to the smaller id, so a call is repeatable. The digest is `{id, category, title, claim ≤ 200 characters cut at a word with "…", language, version}`. The "angles" focus filter is not applied: cards have no angle, ideas do.
  - **MMR cost:** the highest similarity to the picked set is updated after each pick, so choosing 60 of N cards costs 60 × N dot products instead of 60 × N × 60.
  - **`searchApproved(ctx, {query, limit, categories, language, minSimilarity})`:** embeds the query (purpose `query`), orders approved cards by cosine distance, returns digests with `similarity`; an empty query is a `ValidationError` before anything is embedded; limit 1–50.
  - **`getApprovedSnapshots(ctx, [{knowledgeItemId, version}])`:** the frozen text from `knowledge_item_versions`, whatever happened to the card since; `NotFoundError` lists missing versions. Resolving an idea's links is the new follow-up task **M2-06a** (`getIdeaCards(ideaId)` and the "PRIMARY in the last 30 days" lookup that feeds `recentlyUsedIds`), needing M2-01.

## 2026-10-07 · Units and numeric fidelity [M2-04]
- Context: 07 §7.9.3 fixes the °C/°F rounding and the tolerances but leaves the rest of the conversion table, the number formats and what counts as "a match" open.
- Decision (`modules/src/localization/units.ts`, `numeric-fidelity.ts`, pure functions, exported from `@rc/modules/localization`):
  - **Conversions:** °C→°F rounded to 5 for `OVEN`, 1 for every other target; °F→°C follows the same steps (the plan gives only °F). g/kg→oz below one pound (steps of ¼ oz), lb above (0.1 lb); ml/l→a standard cup fraction (¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾ 1 1¼ 1½ 2 2½ 3 4) when within 5 % of it, else fl oz (½ steps); cm→in (¼ steps). The cup is the US legal cup, 240 ml. Back to metric: oz/lb/floz/cup to g or ml in steps of 5 (1 below 50), kg and l in 0.05, cm in 0.5. A conversion that rounds to zero returns `undefined` and the display falls back to the original unit.
  - **Display:** es-ES (METRIC) `180 °C`, decimal comma; en (DUAL) imperial first with the metric original in brackets, `355 °F (180 °C)`, `8.75 oz (250 g)`, `1 cup (250 ml)`; IMPERIAL imperial only. Regular spaces, no non-breaking ones, so validators and regexes see plain text. Times are never converted (`10–15 min`). `buildConversionTable(card, market)` returns, per card number, the card's own string and the string to use; M2-06/M2-09 inject it into the prompts.
  - **Extraction:** numbers followed by a unit, in en/es/ru spellings (°C, ºC, `grados`, g, kg, ml, l, oz, lb, fl oz, cups/tazas, cm, inches, s, min, h, d, %); ranges with `-`, `–`, `a`, `to`; fractions (`½`, `1/2`, `1 1/2`); decimal comma for es/ru and point for en, thousands separators by locale; `1 h 30 min` is one 90-minute number. Numbers without a unit and `in` as a bare unit are ignored ("1 in 5").
  - **Matching:** a number matches when it lies within a reference value's tolerance; a range needs both ends to match. Temperatures ±2 °C or ±5 °F (inclusive), compared in the unit the text uses; bare `180º` may match either. Times exact or inside the card's range (converted to minutes). Mass, volume and length: 1 % (6 % for cups), or half a display step for imperial units (oz ¼, lb 0.1, fl oz ½, in ¼), whichever is larger. Percent exact. Reference values come from the card's structured fields (temperatures, timings, ingredient quantities) and from every number with a unit found in its free text (claim, explanation, steps), because ratios and percentages have no structured field. A mismatch is `NUMERIC_MISMATCH` (BLOCKER) with the field path and the card values of that kind in `fixHint`.
- Known limits: number words ("two hours"), `½ hora`-style words, ranges whose ends carry different units, and unit-less numbers are not checked; a hand-rounded imperial value off the table (`8 inches` for 20 cm, `9 oz` for 250 g) is reported, which sends the writer to repair. Scaling a recipe (doubling servings) is a mismatch by design.
- Evidence / links: `modules/src/localization/units.test.ts` and `numeric-fidelity.test.ts` (table-driven: every conversion, es-ES and en formatting, extraction in three languages, tolerance edges, ranges, compound durations).

## 2026-10-08 · Variant generation pipeline [M2-13]
- Context: 07 §7.6.2 gives the flow, 06 J5 the locking and failure rules; resume, the rewrite loop, the order of the markets and the failure cases needed concrete definitions. The `generate-content` job and the actions are M2-14; this task is the pipeline function.
- Decision (`modules/src/content/pipeline/{generate-variants,context,inputs}.ts`): `generateVariants(ctx, { masterIdeaId, variantIds, pipelineRunId, instruction? })`.
  - **Locking:** the idea must be ACCEPTED; every variant must belong to it. A variant that another run holds (GENERATING with another `pipelineRunId`) stops the call before any variant is touched (`InvalidStateError`). DRAFT, READY_FOR_REVIEW and CHANGES_REQUESTED variants become GENERATING through `transition()` with a fresh `pipeline_state` and cleared flags and `last_error`; a variant GENERATING for the same run id is resumed. APPROVED and later statuses are refused.
  - **Order:** markets are planned one after another in a shuffle seeded by the run id (the same id gives the same order, so a resumed run plans as before); a later plan sees the earlier plans, and the plans of the idea's other live variants outside the run, as siblings. Drafts are written in parallel, then reviewed in parallel.
  - **Resume:** each stage has a key (`ADAPT`, `WRITE:k`, `CRITIC:k`); after it succeeds its run id goes into `pipeline_state.runIds` and the key into `completedStages`. A finished stage is read back from `generation_runs.output`, so a rerun with the same id repeats no model call. Differentiation and the deterministic validators are recomputed (they cost nothing). A `TransientError` leaves the variants GENERATING for the retry; any other error fails the still-running variants (DRAFT + `GENERATION_FAILED`) and is rethrown. Generation runs are linked to the idea and the variant (`master_idea_id`, `content_variant_id`) after each stage; `runStage` itself only writes `input_refs`.
  - **Critic loop:** differentiation of all live drafts (the idea's other variants first, then this run's in generation order), then critics in parallel and `decideVerdict` with `rewritesDone = iteration`; variants with REQUEST_REWRITE get the writer again with `rewrite { instructions, previous }`, up to `MAX_REWRITES` (2); a draft that passed is not reviewed again. The later market of a failing pair alone is asked to change.
  - **Failures:** a variant whose plan or draft stays invalid after the repair (or is refused) goes back to DRAFT with `GENERATION_FAILED`, `last_error { code INVALID_OUTPUT | MODEL_REFUSED, details.stage }` and no pipeline state; the other markets go on. A critic that cannot give a valid review does **not** cost the draft: it goes to READY_FOR_REVIEW as FLAG_FOR_HUMAN with "The automatic review failed" and no quality score.
  - **Persisted:** hook, hook type, caption, CTA (text, type, JSON), hashtags, slides with stable ids (the ids are kept by index across rewrites), `template_sequence`, `content_length`, the market brief, `offer_id` (the market's highest-priority ACTIVE offer of the idea's product), flags, critic verdict, report (field paths rewritten from slide indexes to slide ids, 04 §4.4 grammar), quality score, the worst differentiation report, `generation_version` (`p1.0.0`), `generation_config` (stage prompts, models, templates version, embedding model), `lock_version + 1`; audit `variant.generation_started` / `generated` / `generation_failed`.
  - **`DUPLICATION_RISK`** is recomputed from the final comparison, so a risk that a rewrite removed does not stay on the earlier market (found by the test).
  - `instruction` (a person's note) goes to the first draft as `rewrite.instructions`.
- Not done: the job, the actions and the statuses poll (M2-14), voice examples and exemplars (the writer gets none until M2-17), visual generation (M3).
- Evidence / links: `modules/src/content/pipeline/generate-variants.test.ts` (15 integration cases with a scripted model: happy path with every stored column and 6 logged runs, sibling plans, rewrite then pass, flag after two rewrites with slide-id paths, rewrite of the later market only on a differentiation FAIL, sibling-copy plan failing, crash and resume with no repeated calls, failure to DRAFT and a clean retry, permanent error, unusable critic, instruction, locking).
- Impact on plan: none.

## 2026-10-08 · Critic prompt v1 and verdict policy [M2-12]
- Context: 07 §7.6.3 lists seven policy rules and 07 §7.6.4 the critic's rules; the order of the rules, what counts as "left after 2 rewrites", the field-path form and the empty-instructions case were open.
- Decision (`prompts/src/critic/`, `modules/src/content/pipeline/`):
  - **Prompt `critic@1`** (stage CRITIC, effort high, 16k tokens, in `STAGE_CONFIG`): checks factual fidelity (each unsupported claim with its exact field path), source coverage, localization, originality against the sibling summary and the differentiation report, brand voice, structure, CTA and safety; whole-number scores 1–5; three verdicts with what each must carry. The input prints a `path` on every part of the draft (`hook`, `slides.2.slots.body`, `caption`, `cta`, `hashtags.0`, `claimsUsed.1`), so the critic quotes paths that exist; the paths use the slide's index, not its id (M2-13 maps them to stored slide ids). The deterministic issues are given as "already found". The output is `CriticReport` without `iteration` and `deterministicIssues`; the score range is checked by `validateCriticOutput` (BLOCKER `SCORE_OUT_OF_RANGE`, `REWRITE_WITHOUT_INSTRUCTIONS`, `FLAG_WITHOUT_ATTENTION`, `FIELD_PATH_UNKNOWN` for a claim; MAJOR `PASS_WITH_UNSUPPORTED_CLAIMS`; MINOR unknown path of an ordinary issue).
  - **Policy `decideVerdict`** (pure): rules 1–3 give REQUEST_REWRITE while fewer than `MAX_REWRITES` (2) rewrites are done: an unsupported claim, a deterministic BLOCKER, a differentiation FAIL **for the market generated later**, any score ≤ 2 (not counting `overall`, which has its own rule) or `overall` < 3. Rule 4 applies only when no rewrite is due: the critic's FLAG_FOR_HUMAN then stands (a rewrite is cheaper than a person, and the next review can still ask for one). Rule 5: when rules 1–3 still fire after the 2 rewrites the verdict is FLAG_FOR_HUMAN with flags from what is left (`UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM`, `NUMERIC_MISMATCH`, `TEXT_OVERFLOW` … via `flagsForIssues`, `DUPLICATION_RISK`). Rule 6: `SAFETY_REVIEW` is added to every verdict when a cited card is safety-sensitive. Rule 7: `qualityScore` = (2 × factual + 1.5 × localization + the six others) / 9.5, two decimals, clamped to 1–5.
  - **Choices beyond the plan:** a BLOCKER issue found by the critic itself also triggers a rewrite (a safety problem is not an "unsupported claim"); a model REQUEST_REWRITE that no rule backs is ignored (the policy is deterministic); on a final verdict `DUPLICATION_RISK` is set for any differentiation WARN or FAIL, for both markets of the pair, and the later market alone is asked to rewrite; flags other than `SAFETY_REVIEW` are set only on a final verdict (PASS or FLAG_FOR_HUMAN), never on a REQUEST_REWRITE; rewrite instructions are the critic's text, one line per unsupported claim, per blocking validation issue (its fix hint) and per blocking critic issue, the fixed differentiation line "Change the hook type and the slide structure; keep the facts.", the critic's MAJOR fixes, and the reasons when nothing else exists, so a rewrite never goes out empty.
  - `buildCriticReport` turns the model's output and the decision into the stored `CriticReport` (the policy's verdict, blank fields dropped).

## 2026-10-08 · Cross-market differentiation checker [M2-11]
- Context: 07 §7.10 gives the three scores, the FAIL and WARN rules and placeholder thresholds (A-18); the code shape, the order of a pair and the rounding were open.
- Decision (`modules/src/localization/differentiation.ts`):
  - **Scores** exactly as 07 §7.10: `hookSimilarity` (cosine of the hooks), `slideTextSimilarity` (symmetric mean of the best-match cosine between the slide texts; a slide's text is its slot texts joined in slot order, blank slides are skipped), `templateSequenceSimilarity` (1 − Levenshtein / longer length over the template ids; two empty sequences count as 1), `sameHookType`. All in 0–1 (negative cosines count as 0), rounded to four decimals.
  - **Verdict:** FAIL when hook ≥ 0.90, slide text ≥ 0.88, or the template sequence is identical **and** the hook type is the same; WARN when hook ≥ 0.85, slide text ≥ 0.82 or the sequence similarity ≥ 0.8; otherwise OK. `reasons` lists each finding in words; the worst verdict wins.
  - **Visual prompts** are compared the same way when both variants have image prompts and give only a WARN at ≥ 0.90 (the plan names no threshold; there is no data before M3).
  - **Thresholds are a config object** (`DIFFERENTIATION_THRESHOLDS`, version `d1-placeholder`) passed to every function and recorded as `thresholdsVersion` in each report, so Gate G1 can recalibrate by changing one object and its version.
  - **API:** `differentiateVariants(embeddings, variants)` embeds every hook, slide text and image prompt in **one** provider call and returns a report per pair (`a`, `b`, `report`); callers pass the markets in generation order, so `b` is the one generated later and is the one the policy of M2-12 asks to change on a FAIL (07 §7.6.3). `scoreDifferentiation` works on ready vectors (pure), `worstReport` picks the worst pair for a variant with several siblings.
- Not done: storing the report on `content_variants` and the repair loop (M2-13), the policy that turns FAIL into REQUEST_REWRITE (M2-12), calibration on labelled pairs (G1).
- Evidence / links: `modules/src/localization/differentiation.test.ts` (19 cases: boundaries of every threshold on controlled vectors, a translation forced similar with the fake provider → FAIL, a true localization → OK, same hook type and templates with different words → FAIL, template sequence distance, one embedding call, pairs, visual prompts).

## 2026-09-27 · Crypto utilities [M0-07]
- Context: 12 §12.3 defines token encryption, OAuth state and Meta `signed_request` checks.
- Decision:
